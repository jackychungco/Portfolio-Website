PUT YOUR INTERIOR PHOTOS IN THIS FOLDER.

1. Drag your photos in here.
2. Rename each one: lowercase, no spaces, use dashes.
      GOOD -> living-room-01.jpg
      BAD  -> Living Room (1).JPG
3. Open content.js (one level up) and list the filenames in the
   INTERIORS section at the bottom.

The interior-01.jpg ... interior-06.jpg files in here right now are
grey PLACEHOLDERS so the page isn't empty. Delete them once your real
photos are in.

SIZE: keep each photo under about 2 MB, longest edge around 2000 pixels.
Anything bigger just makes the site slow to load. On a Mac you can
resize by opening the photo in Preview, then Tools > Adjust Size.
